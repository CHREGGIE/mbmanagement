## Block 5 — FAQ accordion (`#frequently-asked-questions`)

**H2:** Your Questions, Answered

> Replace all three items and add two. The old answers belonged to different questions.

**Q: What services are included in the management fee?**
A: Full-service management: leasing and tenant placement, tenant screening, rent collection and owner disbursements, maintenance coordination, property inspections, lease renewals, and monthly financial reporting with year-end statements. Our fee is a percentage of collected rent, and we disclose every fee upfront, with no surprise charges. [Contact us](/contact-us) for a proposal specific to your property.

**Q: What is your tenant screening process?**
A: Every applicant goes through a credit check, a criminal background check, income verification (typically 2.5–3x the monthly rent) and references from previous landlords. The goal is a tenant who is financially qualified and has a clean rental history.

**Q: How do you handle evictions in CT?**
A: We manage the full process on your behalf, from formal notice and documentation to coordinating with Connecticut eviction attorneys and representing your interests through Housing Court.

**Q: Can you manage my property if I live out of state?**
A: Yes. We work with out-of-state owners often. Through our online portal you can see financial statements, maintenance records and inspection reports at any time.

**Q: Can you take over a property I'm managing myself?**
A: Yes. We notify your tenants, collect the existing lease documents, update payment instructions and run an initial inspection to set a baseline condition report.
